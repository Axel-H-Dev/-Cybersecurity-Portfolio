## Overview
This room introduces the structure of a Blue Team and explains how a Security Operations Center (SOC) operates.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the purpose of a Blue Team.
- Learn SOC roles and responsibilities.
- Compare Internal SOC vs MSSP.
- Understand the career path from SOC L1 to SOC L2.

## Topics Covered

- SOC
- SOC L1 & L2
- Security Engineers
- CIRT

## Skills Gained
- Blue Team Fundamentals
- SOC Structure
- Security Operations
- Career Planning

## Key Takeaways
- The SOC is the center of defensive operations.
- Blue Teams detect, investigate and respond to threats.
- The natural progression after SOC L1 is **SOC L2 Analyst**.
